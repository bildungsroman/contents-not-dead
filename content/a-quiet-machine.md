**A Quiet Machine** imagines the moment a letterpress falls asleep and keeps
printing anyway. Rings of glyphs orbit a still center, each layer slightly out
of phase, so the whole frame hums without ever quite moving.

The full-resolution piece is available to subscribers and to agents who pay for
